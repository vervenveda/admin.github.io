One Door Security and Privacy Notes

Hosting

Use One Door locally or from a genuinely private administrative environment.

Do not rely on an unlisted URL, a client-side passphrase prompt, or public GitHub Pages as access control.

Device controls

Use the workspace only on a trusted device with:

A strong operating-system login

Full-disk encryption

Automatic screen locking

Current browser and operating-system updates

Restricted physical access

Approved backup handling

Workspace passphrase

The optional passphrase encrypts browser storage. It is never transmitted and cannot be recovered by the application.

Use a unique passphrase of at least 12 characters.

Backups

Private backups contain the complete workspace in readable JSON. Store them only in an approved encrypted location.

Do not email backups or place them in a public repository.

Data minimization

Collect only the minimum information needed for service coordination. Avoid document numbers, account credentials, detailed diagnoses, and unnecessary personal history.

Exports

Review every client, referral, or internal export before printing, downloading, or sharing.

Confirm:

Active consent

Correct recipient

Necessary scope

Safe contact details

No internal-only notes

No unnecessary sensitive information

Emergencies

One Door is not an emergency-dispatch, crisis-response, medical-record, legal-advice, or law-enforcement system. Follow approved local emergency and mandatory-reporting procedures.

Naming

“One Door” is used descriptively and without a trademark claim. This document is not a legal clearance opinion.
